# 4 West data

All data is fictional and created for MHS 510 at Atlantis University. Bayfront Palms Medical Center, its 4 West unit, and every person named in these files are invented; no real patient, staff member, or hospital is described. The files were generated by a seeded script so that every student works with the same numbers. Download any file and open it in Excel or Google Sheets.

Dates: the census and incident files cover 2026-04-01 to 2026-09-30, the scorecard covers the fiscal year 2025-10 to 2026-09, the schedule covers 2026-09-03 to 2026-09-30, and the exits in the turnover file run from 2025-04 to 2026-09. Night shift rows carry the date the shift started (7 p.m.), so an incident at 02:00 dated 2026-04-04 happened early on April 5.

## staff_roster.csv

The 48 people who work on 4 West on 2026-09-30: 30 RNs (18 day, 12 night), 4 LPNs, 12 CNAs, and 2 unit secretaries, with tenure, generation, education, certification, charge and preceptor roles, languages, home area, last evaluation score, and formal recognitions in the last 12 months. (48 rows)

Columns: `staff_id`, `name`, `role`, `shift`, `employment`, `fte`, `hire_date`, `years_on_unit`, `years_in_nursing`, `birth_year`, `generation`, `education`, `certification`, `charge_nurse`, `preceptor`, `languages`, `home_area`, `last_eval_score`, `recognition_last_12mo`

## engagement_survey.csv

41 anonymous responses to the September 2026 unit engagement survey: ten original items on a 1 to 5 scale (1 strongly disagree, 5 strongly agree) and an engagement index. Item 10 (burnout) is reverse-scored: the index is the mean of items 1 to 9 plus (6 minus item 10). (41 rows)

Columns: `response_id`, `role`, `shift`, `tenure_band`, `generation`, `q01_recognized`, `q02_manager_support`, `q03_workload_manageable`, `q04_teamwork`, `q05_voice`, `q06_growth`, `q07_safe_to_speak`, `q08_handoffs`, `q09_intent_to_stay`, `q10_burnout`, `engagement_index`

The items: q01 "I am recognized when I do good work"; q02 "My manager supports me when problems arise"; q03 "My workload is manageable on most shifts"; q04 "People on this unit help each other"; q05 "I have a say in decisions that affect my work"; q06 "I have opportunities to grow here"; q07 "I can raise a concern without fear"; q08 "Handoffs between shifts give me what I need"; q09 "I plan to be working on this unit in two years"; q10 "I feel emotionally exhausted at the end of most shifts".

## survey_comments.csv

120 free-text comments from the same survey, in English and Spanish, without theme or sentiment labels. (120 rows)

Columns: `comment_id`, `role`, `shift`, `comment`

## schedule.csv

Four weeks of the 4 West schedule (2026-09-03 to 2026-09-30), one row per person per shift worked, both shifts. The charge nurse of each shift has the role Charge RN and is counted outside the patient ratio. Contract staff who are not unit employees appear with the ids AGENCY and FLOAT. `hours` are paid hours for the shift; `overtime_hours` are the hours paid at the overtime rate (a whole extra shift, or the time a nurse stayed past 12 hours). (597 rows)

Columns: `date`, `day_of_week`, `shift`, `staff_id`, `name`, `role`, `hours`, `overtime_hours`, `source`

## daily_census.csv

Six months of census and staffing, one row per date and shift (Day is 7 a.m. to 7 p.m., Night is 7 p.m. to 7 a.m.). `rn_on_shift` counts bedside RNs (the charge nurse excluded) and matches the schedule for the dates both files cover. `patients_per_rn` = census / rn_on_shift; `nursing_hours` = 12 x (RNs + CNAs + LPNs on the shift); `hppd` (hours per patient day) = nursing_hours / census. (366 rows)

Columns: `date`, `day_of_week`, `shift`, `census`, `admissions`, `discharges`, `rn_on_shift`, `cna_on_shift`, `call_outs`, `patients_per_rn`, `nursing_hours`, `hppd`

## incidents.csv

Six months of incident reports on 4 West: falls, medication errors, pressure injuries, handoff misses, patient complaints, staff injuries, and incivility reports, each with time, severity, room (401 to 432), the contributing factor assigned by the reviewer, and a one-sentence description. (96 rows)

Columns: `incident_id`, `date`, `shift`, `time_of_day`, `type`, `severity`, `room`, `contributing_factor`, `description`

## unit_scorecard.csv

The monthly 4 West scorecard for the fiscal year 2025-10 to 2026-09: patient days, nurse-sensitive indicators per 1,000 patient days, HCAHPS top-box percentages, RN turnover and vacancy, overtime and agency use, length of stay, the engagement index, and the hospital benchmarks (falls 2.5 per 1,000 patient days, nurse communication 78 percent). RN turnover is annualized from the trailing 12 months of separations; vacancy compares filled RN positions with the 35 budgeted. (12 rows)

Columns: `month`, `patient_days`, `falls`, `falls_per_1000_pt_days`, `hapi`, `hapi_per_1000_pt_days`, `med_errors_per_1000_doses`, `hcahps_nurse_communication_topbox_pct`, `hcahps_responsiveness_topbox_pct`, `rn_turnover_annualized_pct`, `rn_vacancy_pct`, `overtime_pct_of_hours`, `agency_hours`, `avg_los_days`, `engagement_index`, `hospital_benchmark_falls`, `hospital_benchmark_hcahps_comm`

## turnover.csv

The 26 people who left 4 West between April 2025 and September 2026, with hire and exit dates, tenure in months, the exit reason recorded by Human Resources, whether they would return, and one sentence from the exit interview. (26 rows)

Columns: `staff_id`, `name`, `role`, `shift`, `hire_date`, `exit_date`, `tenure_months`, `exit_reason`, `would_return`, `exit_comment`

## unit_budget.csv

Budget and actual spending for 4 West by month and category for the fiscal year 2025-10 to 2026-09 (12 months x 9 categories). `variance` is actual minus budget; a positive number means overspent. The Overtime premium line carries all pay for overtime hours; Agency staffing is agency hours x $95. (108 rows)

Columns: `month`, `category`, `budget`, `actual`, `variance`

## stakeholders.csv

The 14 people and roles that matter for the bedside shift report change on 4 West, with influence (1 low to 5 high), current support (-2 strongly against to +2 champion), main concern, and what each person values. (14 rows)

Columns: `stakeholder`, `role`, `influence`, `current_support`, `main_concern`, `what_they_value`

## Budget and staffing facts

Use these facts, with the files above, for every cost calculation in the course.

- RN base pay: $41 per hour.
- One 1.0 FTE (full-time equivalent) = 2,080 paid hours per year, so one 1.0 FTE RN costs $41 x 2,080 = $85,280 per year in base pay.
- Benefits add 28 percent: $85,280 x 1.28 = $109,158 per year for one 1.0 FTE RN.
- Overtime is paid at 1.5 x base pay: $61.50 per hour for an RN.
- An agency RN is billed at $95 per hour.
- Recruiting and onboarding one RN costs about $62,000 (advertising, orientation, preceptor time, and agency coverage while the position is vacant).
- Target staffing on 4 West: 1 RN to 5 patients on days, 1 RN to 6 on nights, one CNA to 8 to 10 patients, one charge nurse per shift outside the ratio.
- A full-time 4 West nurse works three 12-hour shifts a week (0.9 FTE); part-time is 0.6 FTE and per diem 0.1 FTE in the roster.
