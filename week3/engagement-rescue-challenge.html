Engagement rescue challenge

The CNO lets you redirect underspent budget to the night shift for one quarter, and you have ten manager hours a week. Build the bundle of actions that lifts the night survey the most, with the right theory behind each card. Four rounds, each with a different constraint, 25 points each.

How to use this interactive
  1. Read the round: its budget, its manager hours, and the four survey items it asks you to move.
  2. Tap cards to add them to your bundle. Each card shows its cost for the quarter, its manager hours a week, its theory, and the items it touches. The budget and hours gauges update, and a packet shows the move on the floor.
  3. Press Submit bundle. The board on the floor shows the predicted lift, and the panel scores coverage (10), lift against the best feasible bundle (10), and balance between motivators and hygiene (5). Overspending the budget or the hours costs you the lift and balance points.
  4. Read every card's real effect in the table, including the cards you did not pick, then go to the next round. Four rounds make 100 points. Try again reshuffles the cards.

How a round is scored 25 points per round

PartPointsWhat earns them
Coverage102.5 for each of the round's four target items that at least one chosen card moves. The targets come from the lowest night items in the survey file.
Lift10Your predicted gain in the night engagement index compared with the best gain any bundle can reach inside the round's budget and hours while covering all four targets with both motivators and hygiene. Within 10% of that best earns the full 10. A second card on the same item adds less (60%, then 40%): diminishing returns.
Balance53 if the bundle holds at least one motivator (Herzberg: recognition, growth, the work itself) and 2 if it also holds a hygiene or process card. A hygiene-only bundle removes irritants but does not build engagement.
Overspend0If the bundle costs more than the budget or more manager hours than you have, lift and balance score 0 for the round. Coverage still counts.

The predicted effects are the course model's estimates, built from the survey gaps (recognition +1.11 and manager support +1.01 between staff who intend to stay and those who do not), the turnover file (Schedule and Relationship with charge nurse lead the night exit reasons), and the theories of Week 3. Costs use the budget facts: RN $41 an hour, agency $95 an hour, a $2 differential for 12 night RNs at 36 hours a week is $11,232 a quarter.
