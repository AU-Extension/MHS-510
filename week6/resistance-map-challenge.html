Resistance map challenge

Ten people and groups from 4 West push back on bedside shift report, one at a time. For each, name the reason behind the resistance, choose the manager's response, and place the person on the influence and support grid. Each round is worth 10 points: 3 for the reason, 4 for the response (2 for a defensible second), 3 for the placement (1 if one cell off). Final score out of 100.

Where it happens

The 3D unit floor needs WebGL and the 3D libraries, which are not available here. The flat floor plan shows the same rooms and people, and the round card below has every value.

How this challenge works

  1. Read what the person says in the round card below. On the floor above, that person and their room are lit in coral. Click a person on the floor to see who they are.
  2. Answer question 1: click the main reason behind the resistance. Then answer question 2: click the manager's best response. The table Reasons for resistance and the response that matches, under the round card, is a quick reference.
  3. Answer question 3: click the cell of the grid where this person sits. Influence (1 to 5) goes up the side and support (-2 against to +2 champion) goes across.
  4. Press Check round. Your points and the reason appear under the answers. Green is the best answer, amber earns partial points, coral earns none. On the grid, the green ring marks the right cell. Press See it on the board to watch the person's support change above.
  5. Press Next round. After round 10, press See the final score, review the table, and press Try again (shuffled) if you want a new order.
