Style match challenge

Ten situations from 4 West. For each one, pick the leadership style that fits best and the reason the theory gives. A defensible second choice earns partial credit. Final score out of 100.

How to use this interactive
  1. Read the situation. The floor shows where it happens and who is involved; orbit, zoom, or click a room or a person.
  2. Pick one of the seven styles. The best fit earns 6 points; a defensible second choice earns 3.
  3. Pick the reason the theory gives for that choice. The right reason earns 4 points.
  4. Press Check, read the feedback (it uses the theory's own terms and the unit's data), then Next round.
  5. After ten rounds you get a score out of 100 and a table of your answers. Try again reshuffles the rounds and the options.
