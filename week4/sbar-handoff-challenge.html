How this challenge works

  1. Read the original message in the coral box in the round card below, and who sent it to whom. On the board above, the amber room is the patient the message is about, next to the sender and the receiver.
  2. Under Pieces to choose from, click the pieces in the order the structure needs (for SBAR: Situation, Background, Assessment, Recommendation). Each piece moves to 1. Your message, in order. Two pieces in every pool are noise: true but useless, or wrong. Click a piece in your message to take it out.
  3. Under 2. Which element did the original leave out or get wrong?, click one element.
  4. Press Submit. You earn 1.5 points for each correct piece (6), 2 for the right order, and 2 for naming the missing element, minus 1 for each piece of noise you kept.
  5. Press ▶ Play under the board to watch your message travel, then the rebuilt one. Read the risk the original created, tied to the unit's incident file.
  6. Press Next round ▶. After round 10 your total out of 100 appears in the round card. Press Try again to play the ten rounds again with the pieces reshuffled.
Do this nowRead the original message in the coral box in the round card below.
  • Words to know: SBAR stands for Situation (what is happening now), Background (the context that matters), Assessment (what you think is going on), and Recommendation (what you want done). The structures table at the bottom of the page lists the other two structures.
  • Noise is a piece that does not help the receiver act. A closed loop means the receiver reads the message back and the sender confirms it.
  • Drag on the board to turn it. Use +, −, the arrows, and FIT on the board to look closer; FIT shows the whole unit again. The room under the board jumps to this round's room.
  • Keys: Space steps forward, B steps back, R plays or pauses.
