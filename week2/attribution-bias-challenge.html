Attribution bias challenge

Ten judgments a 4 West manager might make about the staff. Name the bias at work (or say there is none), pick the question to ask before concluding, and check your reasoning against the unit's data. Final score out of 100.

How to use this interactive
  1. Read the manager's judgment and the line of context under it. The floor marks where the evidence lives.
  2. Name the bias: fundamental attribution error, self-serving bias, halo effect, stereotyping, confirmation bias, or no bias. The right name earns 6 points.
  3. Pick the question the manager should ask before concluding. The right question earns 4 points.
  4. Press Check. The feedback explains the bias and shows the evidence from the course data files.
  5. After ten rounds you get a score out of 100 and a table of your answers. Try again reshuffles the rounds and the options.
