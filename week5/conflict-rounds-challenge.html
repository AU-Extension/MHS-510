Conflict rounds challenge

Ten conflicts from 4 West, one at a time. For each, choose the conflict-handling mode, the first sentence the manager says, and the follow-up action. Each round is worth 10 points: 4 for the mode (2 for a defensible second choice), 3 for the sentence, 3 for the action (1 for a partial answer). Final score out of 100.

How to use this interactive
  1. Read the case. The floor shows where it happens and who is involved; orbit, zoom, or click a person to see their role.
  2. Pick the mode that fits the first move (competing, collaborating, compromising, avoiding, accommodating).
  3. Pick the first sentence the manager says, then the follow-up action for the days after.
  4. Press "Check round". The feedback names the theory, cites the data file where one applies, and shows the scene resolving on the floor.
  5. After ten rounds, read your score and the round table, then try again: the rounds and the options are shuffled each time.

Where it happens

The 3D unit floor needs WebGL and the 3D libraries, which are not available here. The flat floor plan shows the same rooms and people, and the panel has every value.
